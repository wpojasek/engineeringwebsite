SpaceTeam AGH

SpaceTeam AGH is a student scientific club at AGH University of Science and Technology focused on space engineering and lunar resource utilization. The team has achieved 1st place in the Over Dusty Moon Challenge 2023 and 2nd place in 2022. Currently, we are working on DISTOBEE, a mobile lunar regolith collection and processing system designed for efficient excavation, transport, and sieving of lunar soil under low gravity and extreme conditions, meeting ESA and ESRIC challenge requirements. I serve on the board, contribute to the mechanical section developing project hardware, and lead the marketing section of the team.
